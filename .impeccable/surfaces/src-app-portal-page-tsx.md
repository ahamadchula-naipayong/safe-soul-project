# Surface Brief: Member Sanctuary Portal

## Scope and Visitor Mode
- Primary target: `src/app/portal/page.tsx`
- Visitor Mode: `Operate`
- Audience: Logged-in members (Free Member, Supporter, Founding 100 Lifetime).

## Outcome and Proof
- Provide a clean, serene, and highly functional private portal where members can see their verified membership tier badge, stream audio masterclasses, download personalized watermarked E-books, access private Discord/Telegram invites, and review saved personal reflection notes.
- Seamless tier switching preview so users can inspect perks and upgrade in one click.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A sacred member dashboard that rejects noisy SaaS user account layouts with generic metric cards in favor of a serene, contemplative haven where digital asset ownership, unlocked chapters, community connections, and spiritual reflection notes coexist in quiet, dignified harmony.

### OWN-WORLD
Deep Emerald (`#08110D`, `#0F291E`, `#12251D`), warm brushed gold accents, parchment cream typography, subtle 1px border geometry, and state-rich emerald/gold badges.

### STORY
The authenticated member arrives to resume their study or retrieve their assets; they immediately see their verified tier prestige, jump back into their unlocked audio chapters, download their personalized watermarked master E-book, review their stored reflection journal, or connect their Discord/Telegram accounts.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Member profile sanctuary header with avatar, name, and verified tier prestige badge (Founding 100 gold / Supporter emerald / Free parchment), followed by quick action cards (Audio Suite, Master E-book, Discord/Telegram Hub, and Reflection Journal).

### FORM
Operate dashboard with tabbed sanctuary sections; seed: none (unavailable)

### FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
