# Surface Brief: Article Reader Sanctuary

## Scope and Visitor Mode
- Primary target: `src/app/articles/[slug]/page.tsx`
- Visitor Mode: `Operate` & `Read`
- Audience: Seekers and students engaging in contemplative reading and study.

## Outcome and Proof
- Provide a distraction-free, contemplative reading haven (Sanctuary Reader) with font size adjustments, focus mode toggle, ambient soundscape, interactive reflection notes, and instant dual-mode switching.
- Clear tier access control with dignified locked state and direct unlock pathway.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
A sacred digital sanctuary reader that rejects noisy blog layouts and ad-heavy publisher templates in favor of a distraction-free contemplative haven equipped with ambient soundscapes, font scale controls, interactive reflection journaling, and instant dual-depth epistemic toggles.

### OWN-WORLD
Deep Emerald (`#08110D`, `#0B1912`), warm brushed gold accents, parchment cream typography, subtle 1px border geometry, and tactile brass/emerald controls.

### STORY
The reader arrives seeking mental clarity and deep contemplation, selects their preferred font size and ambient focus state, switches effortlessly between everyday analogies (Easy) and theological frameworks (Deep), and records personal reflections that persist in their private sanctuary.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Regal chapter title with sacred dimension badge, breadcrumb navigation, interactive reading toolbar (font scale A-/A+, focus mode toggle, ambient soundscape, and podcast audio player trigger), and immediate lead paragraph.

### FORM
Focused contemplative reader with floating sticky controls; seed: none (unavailable)

### FINISH
unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
